📸 Tips for 98%+ Leaf Accuracy

Pl@ntNet neural network performs best when leaf veins, margins, and petiole details are crisp and clear:

  • 🍃
    Single Leaf Focus: Place a single whole leaf flat on a clean, high-contrast surface (white sheet, neutral table, or palm).
  • ☀️
    Bright Diffused Light: Avoid strong direct sun glare or dark shadows. Morning indirect sunlight works best.
  • 🔍
    Macro Venation: Ensure the leaf edges (serration) and internal veins are sharply in focus before shooting.
  • 🚫
    Avoid Distractions: Do not include coins, whole trees, or multiple overlapping species in the frame.
